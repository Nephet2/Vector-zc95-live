VECTOR ZC95 LIVE 0.9.2 - WINDOWS

1. Right-click the downloaded ZIP and choose Extract All.
2. Open the extracted Vector-ZC95 folder.
3. Double-click Vector-ZC95.exe.

Python is included. Use Chrome or Edge for the interface.
Keep the _internal folder with the EXE and keep its console window open.

See README.md for MFP, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi setup.
The app starts with hardware output stopped. Review the preset ranges and
begin with physical device dials at zero before pressing Start or Play.

UPGRADING: Copy your previous settings-zc95-v3.json beside the new EXE
before launching to retain your settings.
